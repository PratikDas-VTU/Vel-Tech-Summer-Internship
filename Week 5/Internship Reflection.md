# Internship Reflection

Looking back on this 5-week summer internship at Necurity Solutions, the experience has been incredibly transformative, bridging the gap between theoretical cybersecurity concepts and practical, enterprise-level engineering.

## Professional Growth
When I started in **Week 1**, my focus was entirely on foundational skills—setting up isolated virtual labs, mastering Linux command-line operations, and understanding the basics of OSINT and networking protocols. The structured exercises provided a safe environment to learn offensive and defensive penetration testing phases.

In **Week 2**, the reality of enterprise cybersecurity operations became apparent. Reviewing VAPT reports and building vulnerability tracking sheets highlighted a critical bottleneck: quality assurance in technical reporting is tedious, manual, and prone to human error.

By **Weeks 3, 4, and 5**, I was given the opportunity to solve this exact problem by architecting and developing the Compliance Checking Application. Designing this software forced me to elevate my skills significantly. I learned to structure large proprietary datasets, design responsive user interfaces in Python, and apply advanced concepts like computer vision and semantic text matching to automate QA checks.

## Conclusion
This internship taught me that cybersecurity is not just about finding vulnerabilities—it is equally about how efficiently and accurately those vulnerabilities are documented, validated, and communicated to stakeholders. I am leaving this internship with a much deeper understanding of vulnerability management workflows, vastly improved software engineering skills, and the confidence to build automated tools that solve real-world operational bottlenecks.
