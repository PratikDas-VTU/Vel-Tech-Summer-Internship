# Project Demonstration

## Handover & Presentation
With the executable packaged and tested, I presented the final Compliance Checking Application to the engineering and QA teams at Necurity Solutions.

## Demonstration Highlights
* Demonstrated the seamless ingestion of both PDF and DOCX files.
* Showcased the real-time threading capabilities of the dashboard, proving the UI remained responsive during heavy processing.
* Generated a live Compliance Report, proving the tool could successfully catch missing CVSS scores and flag unauthorized vendor logos using the OpenCV branding engine.

## Outcome
The demonstration was successful, proving that manual QA bottlenecks in cybersecurity reporting could be effectively mitigated through programmatic automation.
