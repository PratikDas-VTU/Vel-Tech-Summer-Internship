# Confidentiality & Publishing Rules

This repository serves as a professional portfolio showcasing my engineering experience during the summer internship. However, strict adherence to organizational confidentiality is maintained.

## What is Included
* **High-Level Architectural Concepts**: Explanations of how data flows through the Compliance Checking Application.
* **Technology Stack**: Lists of libraries and frameworks used (e.g., Python, CustomTkinter, OpenCV).
* **Public-Safe Features**: Descriptions of the problems solved and the engineering concepts applied.
* **Recreated Assets**: UI screenshots populated entirely with safe, fictional, or generalized data to demonstrate interface design without exposing client data.

## What is Omitted (Strictly Confidential)
* **Source Code**: No `.py`, `.spec`, or build configuration files are included in this public repository.
* **Proprietary Business Logic**: Exact scoring weights, specific algorithm implementations, and regular expressions used for internal validations are withheld.
* **Internal Datasets (Rules & Knowledge Bases)**: The JSON rulesets containing proprietary acronyms, internal network vendor lists, and specific cybersecurity dictionaries developed during the internship are not published.
* **Company Assets & Client Information**: Real client VAPT (Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing) reports, organizational logos, and any generated compliance output PDFs have been strictly excluded.

*If explicit organizational approval is granted in the future, source code may be made available in a controlled manner.*
