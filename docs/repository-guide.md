## Why is there no source code?
Please review **[Confidentiality Rules](confidentiality.md)**. As an enterprise cybersecurity tool, the underlying source code, proprietary algorithms, and real client datasets are strictly confidential and cannot be published to GitHub. The UI assets provided in `assets/ui/` have been safely recreated or generalized for public viewing.
