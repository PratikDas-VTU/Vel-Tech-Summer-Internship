# Vel Tech Summer Internship (2026)

Welcome to my Summer Internship showcase repository. This journal comprehensively documents my experiences, learnings, and achievements during my 5-week tenure at Necurity Solutions. 

---

## 🏢 Internship Overview
This repository serves as a chronological record of my transition from foundational cybersecurity research to applied enterprise automation. Over the course of 23 working days, I developed a strong baseline in penetration testing workflows and ultimately architected a software solution to solve critical quality assurance bottlenecks in technical reporting.

### About Necurity Solutions
Necurity Solutions Network Security Pvt. Ltd. is a specialized firm offering advanced cybersecurity services, including Vulnerability Assessment and Penetration Testing (VAPT), compliance consulting, and comprehensive security audits for enterprise clients.

### Role & Duration
* **Role**: Cybersecurity Researcher Intern
* **Duration**: 01 June 2026 – 01 July 2026 (5 Weeks / 23 Working Days)
* **Organization**: Necurity Solutions Network Security Pvt. Ltd.
* **Academic Institution**: Vel Tech University

### Objectives
* Establish a secure, isolated lab environment for vulnerability research.
* Understand the phases of penetration testing and enterprise vulnerability management.
* Analyze and standardize complex cybersecurity assessment reports.
* Develop an automated, programmatic solution to replace manual report QA processes.

---

## 📅 Weekly Journey (Week 1 – Week 5)

The internship was divided into distinct phases, documented in detail within the weekly folders:

### [Week 1: Organization, Linux, & Foundational Security](Week%201/README.md)
Organization induction, setting up Oracle VirtualBox, deploying Ubuntu and Kali Linux, and mastering terminal administration via TryHackMe and OverTheWire.

### [Week 2: Vulnerability Research & Security Reporting](Week%202/README.md)
Reviewing 22 VAPT reports, researching CVEs across internal software components, querying the National Vulnerability Database (NVD), and tracking findings in structured Excel sheets.

### [Week 3: Automation Design & Prototyping](Week%203/README.md)
Initial conceptualization of the Compliance Checking Application, document validation research, structuring JSON cybersecurity knowledge bases, and building the first CustomTkinter UI prototype.

### [Week 4: Application Enhancement & Testing](Week%204/README.md)
Continued software development, integrating OpenCV for branding consistency, threading the UI for performance, and conducting extensive testing against sample security reports based on mentor feedback.

### [Week 5: Final Delivery & Conclusion](Week%205/README.md)
The final 3 working days: generating PDF compliance reports, PyInstaller executable packaging, the final project demonstration to the engineering team, and offboarding.

---

## 🚀 Major Internship Work
While the first half of the internship focused on research and manual analysis, the latter half was dedicated to solving an operational problem I identified: the manual QA of VAPT reports is slow and error-prone.

I designed and developed the **Compliance Checking Application**—an automated desktop tool capable of programmatically parsing PDF and DOCX reports, running semantic vulnerability checks, and using computer vision to verify organizational branding. This tool was the capstone achievement of my time at Necurity Solutions.
